About Footprints Energy

Footprints Energy is a leading commodity trader , specializing in agricultural produce, animal protein and energy derivatives. We supply commodities such as Sugar (IC45, VHP) , Animal Protein (Chicken, Bovine and Swine) and Grains (Soy, Corn and Coffee). We also facilitate trade of petroleum derivates such as Jet A4, AGO and PMS.

At Footprints, we understand our markets, follow trends and proactively take measures to act in the best interest of our customers.

How

1. LOI

To get started, please send a Letter of Intent (LOI) outlining the product specifications, required quantity, and destination port. Once we receive this information, we’ll promptly provide you with a formal offer.

2. FCO

Our Full Corporate Offer (FCO) will be tailored specifically to your request and will remain valid for a limited period, as global market prices are subject to change.

3. SPA & Delivery

To finalize your order, a Sales and Purchase Agreement (SPA) outlining the agreed terms and procedures must be signed and executed. Footprints Energy operates under Incoterms and is legally based in the United States.
